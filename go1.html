The path

  1. 1.1What Physical AI and scientific machine learning areScientific machine learning blends physical laws with learning from data. Physical AI puts such models into machines that sense and act in the real world, from robots to automated vehicles.35 min
    Big picture
  2. 1.2Differential equations: the language of physicsA differential equation says how something changes. Solving it, with a formula or step by step with methods such as Euler's, predicts how the system evolves. Step size controls accuracy.40 min
    Foundations
  3. 1.3Neural networks from scratchA neural network is a flexible function built from simple units. Each neuron applies a weighted sum and a nonlinearity; stacked together they can approximate almost any smooth relationship.40 min
    Foundations
  4. 1.4Learning as optimisation: loss, gradients, autodiffTraining a model means minimising a loss by walking downhill along its gradient. Learning rate, momentum and local minima decide whether training succeeds; automatic differentiation computes the gradients.40 min
    Foundations
  5. 1.5Physics-informed learning: the core ideaA physics-informed model is trained to fit the data and to obey a known equation at many points. The physics fills the gaps between and beyond measurements, so far fewer data are needed.40 min
    Core idea
  6. 1.6Data, noise and overfittingReal data are noisy and limited. Models that are too flexible learn the noise; models that are too simple miss the pattern. Judge every model on data it has not seen.35 min
    Foundations
  7. 1.7Surrogates and emulators: fast stand-insA surrogate learns the input–output map of a slow simulator from a few runs, then answers new questions almost instantly. Where you sample and what you assume about smoothness decide its accuracy.35 min
    Methods
  8. 1.8Physical AI: sense, estimate, decide, actPhysical AI closes a loop: sensors measure the world, filters estimate its state, models predict, controllers act, and the world responds. Physics-based estimation such as the Kalman filter is the foundation.40 min
    Physical AI
