Research Frontier: Transportation

Apply SciML and Physical AI to transportation research: traffic state estimation, automated vehicles, road safety, energy and emissions, GCAM emulators, crowd dynamics and digital twins.

Is this go for you?

You need: Go 2, or its exit check. Python; PyTorch for the larger labs. Access to transport data (open datasets such as highD or NGSIM are fine).

How it works: Each module reads like a research brief for one transport application: the problem, the evidence, the method, a working lab, and open questions.

What you will produce

  • A research question and study design for a transport SciML project
  • Working prototypes: physics-informed traffic estimation, platoon simulation, conflict analysis, hybrid emissions, an IAM emulator and a digital twin
  • A results section with baselines, uncertainty and limits

The path

  1. 3.1Physics-informed traffic state estimationDetectors see traffic at a few points; operators need it everywhere. Combining the LWR traffic-flow model with sparse detector data reconstructs jams and shock waves that interpolation misses.50 min
    Traffic
  2. 3.2Car following, automated vehicles and Physical AICar-following models describe how drivers react to the vehicle ahead. Physics-based models, learned residuals and cooperative automation decide whether braking waves fade or grow along a platoon.50 min
    Vehicles
  3. 3.3Road safety: conflicts, time to collision and rare eventsCrashes are rare, so safety studies use near misses. Physics gives surrogate measures such as time to collision; machine learning detects conflicts in video at scale; together they estimate risk before crashes happen.50 min
    Safety
  4. 3.4Sustainability: vehicle energy and emissionsVehicle energy follows from physics: mass, acceleration, grade, rolling resistance and drag. Hybrid models add learned corrections for engines and traffic, giving accurate, explainable emission estimates for policies.45 min
    Energy
  5. 3.5GCAM and integrated assessment emulatorsIntegrated assessment models such as GCAM link energy, land, water, economy and climate, but each run is slow. Emulators trained on a few runs make thousands of policy scenarios and uncertainty studies possible.50 min
    Climate
  6. 3.6Crowd dynamics with scientific machine learningCrowd data are plentiful at comfortable densities and scarce at dangerous ones. Physics-shaped models, learned force parameters and physics-informed networks make crowd predictions trustworthy where it matters.45 min
    Crowds
  7. 3.7Digital twins for transport operationsA transport digital twin runs a model in step with live data: it estimates the current state, forecasts and tests actions safely. Data assimilation keeps it honest; physics carries it through sensor gaps.45 min
    Twins
  8. 3.8The research frontier and responsible SciMLOpen questions: foundation models for physical systems, rare events, verification of learned controllers and fairness. Report SciML results honestly, with baselines, uncertainty and limits.40 min
    Research

An open course on Physical AI and scientific machine learning, taught in three goes, with transportation as the research case. Methods follow the cited literature (Raissi, Perdikaris and Karniadakis; Brunton, Proctor and Kutz; Lu and colleagues; Rasmussen and Williams; Kalman; Treiber and colleagues; among others). Explorers use synthetic teaching data: never use them on their own for engineering or safety decisions.
