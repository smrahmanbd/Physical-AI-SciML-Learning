Learn Physical AI and scientific machine learning three times, each time deeper

Scientific machine learning blends physical laws with learning from data; Physical AI puts the results to work in machines that sense and act. You will meet it three times: first as pictures and simulations, then as tools you build step by step, then as research on transportation: safety, sustainability, GCAM, crowds and digital twins.

Go 1Introductory FoundationsUnderstand what scientific machine learning and Physical AI are, and the building blocks behind them: differential equations, neural networks, optimisation, physics-informed losses, noise, surrogates and the sense–act loop. 8 modules · about 5.1 hours · you need: nothing beyond school mathematics and curiosityGo 2Intermediate Build and ImplementBuild the main SciML tools step by step: an honest workflow, physics-informed neural networks, PDE solvers, equation discovery, neural operators, Gaussian processes, hybrid models and calibrated uncertainty. 8 modules · about 6.4 hours · you need: go 1, or its exit checkGo 3Advanced Research Frontier: TransportationApply SciML and Physical AI to transportation research: traffic state estimation, automated vehicles, road safety, energy and emissions, GCAM emulators, crowd dynamics and digital twins. 8 modules · about 6.2 hours · you need: go 2, or its exit check

How the goes fit together

Start where you are

Each go opens with a three-question check. If you already know the answers, skip ahead. Each go closes with an exit check: tick off what you can do before moving on.

Each go works differently

Go 1 is pictures, animations and predict-then-check simulators. Go 2 is step-by-step builds with code that runs in your browser. Go 3 is a research brief for each transport application, with labs.

Real science, honest toys

Methods come from the cited papers. Explorers and labs use synthetic data with fixed seeds, so every number can be reproduced; they teach the ideas and are not engineering tools.
