Open tools and data

  • PyTorchNeural networks with automatic differentiation; used by the downloadable labs.
  • DeepXDELibrary for physics-informed neural networks and DeepONet, with many examples.
  • PySINDySparse identification of nonlinear dynamics (equation discovery).
  • NVIDIA PhysicsNeMoOpen-source framework for physics-ML models (formerly Modulus).
  • SUMOOpen-source traffic simulator for generating data and building digital twins.
  • highD datasetDrone-recorded vehicle trajectories for car following, safety and estimation studies.
  • GCAMThe open-source Global Change Analysis Model, for emulator studies.

An open course on Physical AI and scientific machine learning, taught in three goes, with transportation as the research case. Methods follow the cited literature (Raissi, Perdikaris and Karniadakis; Brunton, Proctor and Kutz; Lu and colleagues; Rasmussen and Williams; Kalman; Treiber and colleagues; among others). Explorers use synthetic teaching data: never use them on their own for engineering or safety decisions.
