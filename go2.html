The path

  1. 2.1The SciML workflow and toolchainA reliable SciML project follows a fixed workflow: frame the question, gather data and physics, split data honestly, build a baseline, add physics, validate and document. Tools: Python, NumPy, PyTorch or JAX.45 min
    Workflow
  2. 2.2Build a physics-informed neural network, step by stepBuild a PINN for an ODE from scratch: define the network, compute the residual by automatic differentiation, assemble the loss, train with Adam, watch both loss terms, and solve an inverse problem.55 min
    Build
  3. 2.3Solving PDEs: classical solvers and PINNsPartial differential equations are solved on grids by finite differences or volumes, with stability limits on the time step. PINNs solve them without grids. Learn both, and when each wins.50 min
    Build
  4. 2.4Discovering equations from data: SINDy and neural ODEsInstead of assuming the law, find it. SINDy fits a sparse combination of candidate terms; neural ODEs learn the right-hand side of a differential equation with a network.50 min
    Build
  5. 2.5Operator learning: DeepONet and neural operatorsAn operator maps a whole input function to a whole output function. Once trained on example pairs, a neural operator solves new cases of a PDE in milliseconds, across many inputs at once.50 min
    Build
  6. 2.6Gaussian processes and uncertaintyA Gaussian process predicts a value and its uncertainty, widening its error bars far from data. Kernels encode smoothness; marginal likelihood tunes them; calibrated uncertainty is the goal.45 min
    Build
  7. 2.7Hybrid modelling: physics plus learned correctionsA hybrid model keeps a physics model and learns only what it gets wrong. It needs fewer data than pure machine learning and extrapolates far more safely, as long as the physics part is sound.45 min
    Build
  8. 2.8Validation, uncertainty and deploymentBefore a SciML model is used, test it on unseen conditions, check physical consistency, attach calibrated intervals such as conformal predictions, and plan for monitoring, drift and fallbacks in deployment.45 min
    Deploy

An open course on Physical AI and scientific machine learning, taught in three goes, with transportation as the research case. Methods follow the cited literature (Raissi, Perdikaris and Karniadakis; Brunton, Proctor and Kutz; Lu and colleagues; Rasmussen and Williams; Kalman; Treiber and colleagues; among others).
